Prayer

Prayer for Families

• May Jesus be the foundation of our homes, and may love for Jesus be common to all families.

• Despite the waves of our culture crashing against family values, may our families stand firm against the schemes of the enemy and trust Jesus in everything.

• May the desire to share the good news and to be a light to the world grow in our families.

Prayer for Students and Young Adults

As our students go to school, and our young adults go to college or to work…

• May they have the courage to live out their faith with truth and love wherever they go.

• May they be confident that they belong to Christ and use their gifts to build up others.

• May they be part of a biblical community that draws in unbelievers.

Prayer for Children

As our children go back to school…

• May our children walk each day in Your peace and protection, putting on Your armor so they can stand firm against the pressure of culture, temptations and spiritual attacks.

• Give them godly friends, and may Your Holy Spirit guide them in making wise decisions, empowering them to share Your love and truth with those around them.

• From their earliest years, may Jesus capture our children's hearts and lead them to faith in Him. May they deeply understand Your love for them and, in turn, love You with all their heart, soul and strength.
