About us

Mobilizing prayer. Strengthening movements. Seeing disciples multiply.

A short video about us will be here

We are an international prayer movement that unites followers of Jesus, leaders and teams from many countries around one goal: to make prayer an essential part of the life of every disciple of Christ and of every disciple-making movement.

We believe that prayer is not just a ministry or an event, but the foundation of our relationship with God, a source of spiritual strength and a vital part of fulfilling the Great Commission.

Our mission is to inspire, unite and equip believers for continual prayer: from personal life and family to churches and international movements.

We seek to develop simple, reproducible prayer initiatives that help people grow in fellowship with God and help movements grow stronger and multiply.

Our dream is that every disciple of Jesus becomes a person of prayer, and every disciple movement becomes a movement of prayer.

Because prayer is not an addition to the mission. Prayer is an essential part of it.
