The Daniel Fast

"Fasting is not merely going hungry, not merely giving up food, but a feast on God's Word" (R. Foster)

A fast is a period of abstinence when we devote EXTRA TIME and humble our flesh to seek God's knowledge and understanding. It is EXTRA TIME for EXTRAORDINARY prayer!

Heaven takes part in this!
Daniel 10:1–4, 11–14

The beauty of 21 days of prayer and fasting is that they develop a heightened sense of spiritual awareness and focus. This state of alertness spiritually enriches and transforms.

In Daniel's case, the fast was born out of a desire to understand the fullness of God's plan for His people. Even knowing the prophecies of Jeremiah and the Scriptures, he sought to go deeper into God's purposes, both for his own time and for the future.

Likewise, for us, the 21 days of prayer and fasting are not focused on personal needs. They are an opportunity to seek His Word, His will and His guidance, so that we may be filled with the knowledge of His will in all spiritual wisdom and understanding, bearing fruit in every good work and advancing His kingdom in our generation and beyond, until the day of Christ.

It is about drawing near to the mysteries of His plan and allowing Him to reveal His truth and purpose to us.
