Prayer guides

A 21-day spiritual guide is a daily journal that includes Scripture passages to study, the main prayer points for the day and space for your personal notes. In the prayer guide you can write down your conclusions, decisions, insights and how you are going to apply them.

Each prayer guide focuses on a specific theme. The Bible passages are arranged in a deliberate order, so it is best to read them in the order given, even though passages from one book alternate with others.

You are welcome to share the guide: pass it on to those you want to invite to the fast and to those who need it.

All materials are free. To download, please log in or register on the site. Log in · Register
