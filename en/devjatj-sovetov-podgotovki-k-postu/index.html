Nine Tips for Preparing Well for the Fast

Tip #1
Clear and Specific Goals for the Fast

It is important to define the goals of your fast. There should not be many, but they should be clear and specific. Answer the following questions; they will help you determine your goals for the fast:

  • What questions do you have about knowing God?
  • What questions do you have about knowing the Word of God?
  • In which areas of life do you need a clear understanding of God's will? This may concern a particular choice you are facing, or plans for the near future.
  • In which areas of ministry do you need a clear understanding of God's will? These may be ministry tasks, or projects you are involved in and working on.
  • What questions give you no rest, and you are looking for answers to them?
  • What is lacking in your life? It may be a lack of faith, courage, discipline, finances, love, clarity or commitment.
  • Where do you need a breakthrough? Perhaps in family relationships, in business, in multiplying disciples, leaders and churches, in baptisms, or in understanding some other matters.

Tip # 2
Fasting from Food

Decide for yourself personally how you will humble yourself with regard to food and drink during the Daniel Fast. What will you remove from your diet for 21 days? What will you keep for these days?

  • Perhaps you will keep only vegetables, fruit, nuts and various kinds of porridge.
  • Or you will take the "3M" fast: no flour products, no dairy, no meat.
  • Maybe you will give up only sweets, bread and fried food.


Set portion limits. Decide how much of a given food you will allow yourself to eat. For example: 2 eggs a day, 6 tablespoons of porridge, 1-3 pieces of fruit. You may also want to decide how many meals you will have each day.

It is your decision what you will or will not eat and drink in these days, how many meals you will have and when. You know the state of your health, and also where your flesh needs to be humbled.

You must make a written list of what you "can eat" and a list of what you "cannot eat." This is critically important so that later you and your flesh do not bargain with God over a small scoop of ice cream or something else! Clearly mark your boundaries in the table (it is located after all the tips).

Tip # 3
Fasting from More Than Food

Fasting and humility can involve more than food and drink.

Think about what you need to set aside or put off for 21 days in order to have more opportunity to know God and His will deeply. It may be a fast from social media, watching movies, concerts, magazines, shopping trips. Make your decisions and write them down.

Tip # 4
Spiritual Priorities

Determine your list of spiritual priorities for this time. Stop before God three times a day or more to pray to Him and reflect on His word. It is good to make lists of prayer needs. You may decide to get up earlier than usual and spend 2-3 hours studying the Word.

You will experience an extraordinary blessing if you spend at least one hour a day in praise and worship together with your whole family. Prayerfully make a list of your spiritual disciplines for the time of the fast.

Do not be afraid to involve your whole family and children in the Daniel Fast. What is more, you can invite your whole church and even all the churches in your city. Right now, stop and think about whom you want to invite.

Tip #6
Find a Prayer Partner

The study material offered here encourages you to find a prayer partner for this time of fasting. A prayer partner is a trusted person with whom you can share your struggles, ask questions and be accountable.

Tip #7
Recipes

Prepare simple recipes for the time of the fast in advance and share them on our platforms.

Tip # 8
A Daily Prayer Guide

Each day of the guide offers Scripture passages to study, spiritual reflection, the main prayer points for the day, and questions for personal reflection with free space to write down conclusions, revelations or your own decisions.

The Bible passages are arranged in a special logical sequence, so it is best to read them in the order in which they are given, even though some passages from one book alternate with others.

Tip # 9
The Fasting Table

Fill out the "fasting table." An example is given first, followed by free space for your own notes. The table consists of four blocks. Fill it out 5 days before the fast begins; this will help you be well prepared for the time ahead.
