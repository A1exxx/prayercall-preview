Prayer of Confession

O Heavenly Father, You are righteous, and I am not. Like a sheep, I have gone astray. My thoughts are not Your thoughts. My own will has taken over many areas of my life. And this is sin. I confess to You that I am a sinner and I need Your cleansing power in my life.

Shine Your light on my heart and show me every area where I have wandered. As David prayed, so I pray: Search me, God, and know my heart. Try me, and know my thoughts. See if there is any wicked way in me, and lead me in the everlasting way (Psalm 139:23-24).

As You do this, I will confess every sin to You and ask for Your forgiveness. I want to be a clean vessel for honorable use, a vessel in which Your Spirit can live and work.

Cleanse me thoroughly and make me whiter than snow. Let there be no impure thoughts, no unresolved relationships, and no hurts that I have caused.

Right now I open my life before You. Cleanse me from all unrighteousness. Make me righteous before You.
