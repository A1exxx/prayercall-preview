Prayer of Thirst

Lord, You alone are my portion. You are the only inheritance I desire. How pleasant are the boundaries of my inheritance in You! I rejoice in You and in the portion You have given me in life.

Father, show me Your glory. Show me more of Yourself. I want You, not only Your promises. I want Your presence, not only Your gifts. I want Your Spirit, not just the fruit of Your Spirit.

You are my treasure and my reward. Do not let me leave this place without Your clear and majestic presence in my life. I want to know You, to know the power of Jesus' resurrection and the fellowship of His sufferings (Philippians 3:10). Jesus, do not leave me as an orphan, but come to me. I thirst for Your presence.

Let me delight in You and in my relationship with You more than in any earthly things, any achievements, relationships, treasures, ambitions or interests.

Like the man who found treasure hidden in a field, I joyfully sell everything to have You (Matthew 13:44-45). Oh, give me Yourself! I thirst. Let me taste and see that You are good!
