Prayer for God to Be Glorified

Heavenly Father, You alone are King. You alone deserve all the glory on this earth. I deserve none of it. I confess that my ambitions have too often centered on me, my happiness, my control, my reputation and my name. In my old nature, this was my natural way of life.

But I want to change all of that. It is hard, because I do not know what You will say. But I know that You are good and loving. So whatever You say will bring great glory to You and great fulfillment to me. I only want to serve You and make You known.

So, Father, I ask You to show me how to make Your name better known in my life. Glorify Your name through me! Forgive me for asking, "What is Your will for my life?"
Instead I ask: "Show me Your will." Show me what You are doing in this world, in our generation. Let my understanding be grounded in Your Word, not in my own ideas. As You reveal Your will, grant me the privilege of serving You and Your cause in this world. Show me how my life can best serve You and make You most known.

As Samuel prayed as a boy, so I pray: "Speak, Lord, for Your servant is listening" (1 Samuel 3:9). I surrender my dreams, my aspirations and my hopes, to live for You alone. Make me one who makes You famous!
