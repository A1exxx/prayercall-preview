Daniel Fast

Five Truths About the Daniel Fast

1. Daniel Set His Heart on Fasting

Often our life, the needs of ministry, our concern for people and spiritual uncertainty motivate us to withdraw from everything and be alone with God, taking serious fasts, perhaps for a long time. Unfortunately, it is not always possible to get away into solitude or to take a full fast for a week or more. The Daniel Fast, or partial fast, can be a good solution for such a need.

2. The Daniel Fast Lasted 21 Days

Our desire is that we begin the new year in unity with a 21-day fast. Of course, in the future we may use it twice a year for 21 days, or take partial fasts once a month for 10 days, or perhaps we will feel moved to take a partial fast for 40 days, following the example of Moses and Jesus.
Considering the cultural context, we begin the joint Daniel Fast on January 10. This is a time when everyone is already free from all the holidays. It is the start of the year, when it is very important to see and understand God's will, His plans and prospects for us, our families, churches, ministry teams, businesses, our cities and countries for the coming new year. It is a time to make a serious spiritual breakthrough in our lives and in the whole disciple-making movement.

3. Daniel Fasted to Gain Understanding

Daniel could not put together all the pieces of God's plan for the people of Israel. He studied the prophecies of Jeremiah and knew the Scriptures, but he wanted to understand exactly the full plan of God, which concerned not only his own time but the future. This fast is not only about our personal needs; it is a fast to understand His word to us, His will and direction.

4. Daniel Humbled Himself Before God

He ate no pleasant bread; no meat or wine came into his mouth, and he did not anoint himself with ointments for the whole of that time. Fasting is humbling oneself, humbling one's flesh and will. "Now I rejoice in my sufferings for your sake, and fill up on my part that which is lacking of the afflictions of Christ in my flesh for the body's sake, which is the church" (Colossians 1:24)

5. During the Fast Daniel Increased His Spiritual Alertness

Fasting is not just giving something up; it is a special time with God. Daniel prayed every day, at least three times. During the fast he had special supplications and a special immersion in the word of God. The advantage of the Daniel Fast and of a partial fast in general is that it puts us in a position of spiritual wakefulness and special spiritual alertness. It is a very healthy and good feeling.
