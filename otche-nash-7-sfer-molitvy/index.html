The Lord’s Prayer: 7 Areas to Pray For

The Lord’s Prayer is more than a prayer to memorize. Jesus gave us a simple but powerful model for connecting with God and praying over every area of our lives.

In Matthew 6:9–13, we discover seven key areas to include in our daily prayers.

Your Relationship with God

“Our Father in heaven…”

Pray for a deeper relationship with God, stronger faith, a heart that trusts Him, and continued spiritual growth.

God’s Glory

“Hallowed be Your name…”

Pray that God would be honored through your life, your family, and the Church. Pray for more people to come to know Him.

God’s Kingdom and His Will

“Your kingdom come. Your will be done…”

Pray for the Gospel to spread, for pastors and ministry leaders, for your community and nation, and for God’s purposes to be fulfilled.

Your Daily Needs

“Give us this day our daily bread…”

Bring your everyday needs to God—your health, family, work, finances, and the things you’re trusting Him to provide.

Forgiveness and Relationships

“Forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors…”

Ask God to forgive your sins, heal broken relationships, help you let go of bitterness, and give you the grace to forgive others.

Spiritual Strength and Protection

“Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil…”

Pray for strength to resist temptation, freedom from sinful habits, protection from evil, and the courage to stand firm in your faith.

Worship and Thanksgiving

“For Yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever…”

Thank God for His faithfulness, provision, and answered prayers. Praise Him for who He is and trust Him with every part of your life.

Make It Part of Your Daily Prayer

Use these seven areas as a simple guide for your personal prayer time. Bring specific people, needs, and situations before God.

You don’t have to cover everything every time you pray. Let these areas help you grow in your conversations with Him.

The Lord’s Prayer reminds us that prayer isn’t just about asking God for what we need. It’s about inviting Him into every part of our lives.

Based on Matthew 6:9–13.
